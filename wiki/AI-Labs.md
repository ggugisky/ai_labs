# AI Labs

> Source: [[raw/meeting/20260626]]

AI Labs is a biweekly AI sharing and discussion session held on Friday at 5:30 PM for one hour.

## Concept

- Share current AI trends through short agenda items.
- Spread new knowledge across members.
- Support motivation and learning.
- Keep the format open and informal.

## What Can Be Shared

- Harness-related work
- Services currently being built
- Recent AI experiments
- Useful tools, workflows, and patterns
