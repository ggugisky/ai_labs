# AI Labs

> Source: [[raw/meeting/20260626]]

AI Labs is a biweekly AI sharing and discussion session held on Friday at 5:30 PM for one hour.

## Important Distinction

AI Labs is separate from AI Community/AI Committee. AI Labs focuses on informal sharing of personal harnesses, LLM Wiki experiments, AI tools, and productization ideas. AI Community/AI Committee focuses on organization-wide AI adoption, Agentic Engineering guidelines, training, security, Context Foundation, and SAE/Scarlett. Their meeting records must not be mixed.

- AI Labs records: `ai_labs/raw/meeting/`
- AI Community/AI Committee records: `brain/raw/EAK_AI_COMMIT_biweekly_meeting_notes.md` and related Brain notes

## Concept

- Share current AI trends through short agenda items.
- Spread new knowledge across members.
- Support motivation and learning.
- Keep the format open and informal.

## What Can Be Shared

- Harness-related work
- Services currently being built
- Recent AI experiments
- Useful tools, workflows, and patterns
